About MorbMyth Studio

MorbMyth Studio is an automated content management dashboard designed to generate short-form videos (myths, urban legends, and mysteries) and publish them across connected social media channels including TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.

Privacy Policy & Data Usage

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

MorbMyth Studio operates as an internal automated content management tool. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when connected via TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Information We Collect

We request access to basic profile info (user.info.basic) and video publishing permissions (video.publish).

2. How We Use Information

Data is strictly used to authenticate account status on our local dashboard and upload automated short videos to your official TikTok channel. We do not sell, store, or share your personal data with third parties.
